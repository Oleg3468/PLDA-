# PLDA Legal Research Principles

## Source hierarchy
Clearly distinguish:
- Treaties and applicable binding law
- Legislation and regulations
- Court judgments
- Official interpretations and general comments
- Resolutions and recommendations
- Academic commentary and secondary sources

## Source verification
Record, when available:
- Source title
- Issuing institution
- Jurisdiction
- Date
- Effective status
- Edition or version
- Official URL
- SHA-256 hash for stored documents
- Relevant article, paragraph, or section

## Legal analysis
Separate:
- Verified facts
- Applicable legal provisions
- Interpretation
- Counterarguments
- Uncertainty
- Conclusions

## Time
The law applicable to an event may differ from the current law.
Always consider the relevant date and legal version.

## Conflicting sources
Preserve disagreements between agents.
Explain conflicts instead of silently selecting an unsupported answer.
